Common questions

  • My account says pending approval. New doctor accounts are approved by your clinic administrator before first sign-in. Contact your administrator, or email us.
  • An AI response is taking a long time. Responses with several attachments or medication checks can take up to two minutes. Retry with fewer attachments if it times out.
  • A file will not upload. PDFs are accepted up to about 20 MB. Compress or split larger documents.
  • The AI gave an answer I disagree with. AI output is advisory only. Always apply your own clinical judgement — and please report it to us so we can look at it.
