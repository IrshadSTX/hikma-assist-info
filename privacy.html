Hikma Assist

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Hikma Assist is a clinical assistant application for licensed doctors and healthcare professionals. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and what choices you have. It applies to the Hikma Assist iOS and Android applications and the backend services they connect to.

Hikma Assist is not an emergency service. AI output in the app is advisory only and never replaces the judgement of a licensed physician. In an emergency, follow your institutional protocols and contact your local emergency services.

1. Who we are

2. Two kinds of information

The app handles two clearly separate categories, and it matters which is which:

Your account information (you are the subject)

  • Name, email address, and professional details such as department or specialty
  • Profile photo, if you choose to add one
  • Authentication data when you sign in with Google or Apple — we receive your name and email address from them; we never receive your password
  • Basic technical and usage logs (device type, app version, timestamps, error reports) used to keep the service running and secure

Patient information (your patients are the subject)

When you use the app to document care, you enter clinical information about your patients. This can include names, medical record numbers, demographics, allergies, medications, clinical notes, and files you attach — lab reports, radiology and ECG documents, culture and pathology results, prescriptions, clinical photographs, and voice notes you dictate.

For this patient information, you and your clinic are the data controller and we act as a processor on your instructions. We process it to provide the service to you and for no independent purpose of our own.

4. How information is used

  • To provide the core service: patient records, visits, uploads, and the visit timeline
  • To generate AI clinical support — visit summaries, patient summaries, suggested differentials and investigations, and medication safety information
  • To transcribe voice notes you record
  • To keep accounts secure, prevent misuse, and diagnose technical faults

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising or profiling. We do not use patient information to build products for anyone else.

6. Access and separation

Patients are registered under a specific doctor's account. Another doctor using the app cannot see your patients. Access to production data by our staff is limited to what is needed for support and maintenance, and is logged.

7. Security

  • All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit using HTTPS
  • Uploaded files are held on private storage and served through time-limited signed links
  • Clinical messages and uploads are kept as an immutable record for audit integrity
  • Access is authenticated per doctor account, and new accounts require administrator approval

No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we work to protect information using measures appropriate to its sensitivity.

8. Retention

Clinical records are retained for as long as your clinic's account is active, and afterwards for the period required by the medical record retention rules that apply in your jurisdiction. Account information is kept while your account exists. Technical logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted.

9. Your rights and deleting your account

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export, restrict, or delete your personal information, and to object to certain processing.

To delete your account, email hikmaai2030@gmail.com from your registered address, or use Help & Support inside the app. We will confirm your identity and delete your account and personal information within 30 days.

Please note: patient clinical records may need to be retained by your clinic to meet medical record and audit obligations, even after your own account is closed. Where that applies, those records remain under your clinic's control as the data controller, and we tell you what is being kept and why.

10. International transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than your own, including by the providers listed above. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards for the transfer.

11. Children

Hikma Assist is intended for use by licensed healthcare professionals only, and is not directed to children. Patient records entered by a doctor may relate to patients of any age; these are handled as clinical records under the doctor's and clinic's responsibility.

12. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes we will update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
